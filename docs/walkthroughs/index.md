# Walkthroughs

Four step-by-step walkthroughs of the negotiation page, for participants who have never negotiated
and are seeing the page for the first time. Each one has a narrated video.

| Walkthrough | You negotiate | Covers |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| [Against an AI: the basics](han-basics.md) | alone, against an AI opponent | The page, offers, what deals are worth to you, counter-offers, accepting and ending. |
| [Against an AI: advanced](han-advanced.md) | alone, against an AI opponent | The Utility Bar and the other tools, reading the opponent, the deadline, views and settings. |
| [With an Assistant: the basics](hi-basics.md) | with an Assistant, against an AI or a person | Talking to the Assistant, letting it help, playing another person. |
| [With an Assistant: advanced](hi-advanced.md) | with an Assistant, against an AI or a person | How much the Assistant does, its estimate of the opponent, the Assistant in the tool area, choosing the scenario, opponent and Assistant. |

The first two match the Human-Agent Negotiation (HAN) league, where participants negotiate on their
own against submitted AI agents. The last two match the Hybrid Intelligence (HI) league, where
participants negotiate with an Assistant, against AI agents or each other.
